Faster transposed copies of arrays larger than the cache
--------------------------------------------------------
Copies of numeric arrays that change memory order, such as
``np.ascontiguousarray(a.T)``, ``np.asfortranarray(a)``, and assignments
between C- and Fortran-ordered arrays, now visit the strided axis in
cache-sized chunks. The speedup is roughly 3x at 64 MiB on Apple silicon; small
arrays are unaffected.
