``NaT`` now casts to ``NaN`` for floating-point and complex dtypes
------------------------------------------------------------------
Casting ``NaT`` from `numpy.datetime64` or `numpy.timedelta64` to a
floating-point or complex dtype now yields ``NaN``. Previously the cast
used the underlying ``int64`` sentinel, giving ``-9.2e18``, or ``-inf``
with an overflow warning for ``float16``. Casts to integer and boolean
dtypes are unchanged.
