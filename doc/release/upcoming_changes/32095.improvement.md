### Fixed-width string to `StringDType` casts are now safe and validated

The casts from fixed-width {class}`numpy.bytes_` and {class}`numpy.str_` to
{class}`numpy.dtypes.StringDType` are now marked `"safe"` rather than `"same-kind"`. We
also now validate that {class}`numpy.bytes_`, {class}`numpy.void`, and {class}`numpy.str_` arrays
contain valid UTF-8 before converting to {class}`numpy.dtypes.StringDType`.
