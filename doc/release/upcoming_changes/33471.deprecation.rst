``dtype`` argument of ``ndarray.any`` and ``ndarray.all`` is deprecated
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
The undocumented ``dtype`` argument of ``ndarray.any`` and ``ndarray.all`` is
deprecated. Use ``np.logical_or.reduce(..., dtype=..., ...)`` or
``np.logical_and.reduce(..., dtype=..., ...)`` instead.

Until it is removed, ``dtype`` is the second positional argument, so passing
``out`` or ``keepdims`` positionally (e.g. ``a.any(axis, None, out)``) also
warns. Pass them by keyword instead.
